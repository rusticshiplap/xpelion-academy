# Xpelion Academy — New Educational Modules (Complete Collection)

## Overview
This document describes 16 new comprehensive high school modules created across 6 critical subjects. Each module contains:
- **Clear learning objectives** embedded in explanations
- **Conceptual explanations** with real-world analogies
- **3-4 worked examples** with step-by-step solutions
- **6-10 practice quiz questions** (multiple choice) with detailed explanations

## MODULE INVENTORY

### ALGEBRA II (2 modules)

#### 1. **Quadratic Equations & Solutions** (`algebraIIModules`)
- **Topics:** Standard form, quadratic formula, discriminant, completing the square, vertex form, graphing
- **Examples:** Formula application, completing square, complex roots, vertex finding
- **Quiz:** 6 questions covering discriminant interpretation, solving techniques, graphing
- **Depth:** Rigorous treatment of all solving methods with discriminant analysis

#### 2. **Systems of Linear Equations** (`systemsOfEquationsModule`)
- **Topics:** 3×3 systems, elimination method, Gaussian elimination, dependent/inconsistent systems
- **Examples:** Elimination, row operations, dependent systems recognition
- **Quiz:** 4 questions on solution types, Gaussian elimination, rank relationships
- **Depth:** Row-reduction techniques, solution space geometry, error prevention

### GEOMETRY (2 modules)

#### 3. **Transformations & Congruence** (`geometryTransformationsModule`)
- **Topics:** Rigid transformations (translation, reflection, rotation, glide reflection), congruence postulates
- **Examples:** Reflection across lines, 90° rotations, congruence proofs
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on coordinate transformations and rigid motion properties
- **Depth:** Formal treatment of isometries and their properties

#### 4. **Similarity & Circle Theorems** (`similarityModule`)
- **Topics:** Similar figures, scale factors, dilations, inscribed angles, power of a point, tangent segments
- **Examples:** Similar triangle ratios, inscribed angle theorem, power of point calculation
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on area/volume ratios, inscribed angles, tangent properties
- **Depth:** Area ratio = k², volume ratio = k³; circle theorem applications

### PHYSICS (3 modules)

#### 5. **Waves & Wave Motion** (`wavesModule`)
- **Topics:** Wave speed, frequency, wavelength, amplitude, interference, standing waves, Doppler effect
- **Examples:** Wavelength/frequency calculations, standing wave patterns, Doppler frequency shift
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on wave relationships, constructive/destructive interference
- **Depth:** Wave equation v = fλ, harmonic analysis, energy proportionality

#### 6. **Light & Geometric Optics** (`opticsModule`)
- **Topics:** Reflection, refraction, Snell's law, thin lens equation, mirrors, critical angle
- **Examples:** Snell's law calculation, lens imaging, magnification, total internal reflection
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on refraction, lens equations, mirror properties
- **Depth:** Ray optics, lens/mirror equations, image formation analysis

#### 7. **Modern Physics: Relativity & Quanta** (`modernPhysicsModule`)
- **Topics:** Time dilation, mass-energy equivalence (E=mc²), photons, photoelectric effect, Bohr model
- **Examples:** Time dilation factor calculation, rest energy, photon energy, Bohr transitions
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on γ-factor, E=mc² interpretation, photon energy relationships
- **Depth:** Special relativity, quantum photon behavior, atomic structure

### CHEMISTRY (3 modules)

#### 8. **Acids & Bases** (`acidsBasesModule`)
- **Topics:** pH/pOH, strong/weak acids/bases, acid-base titration, buffers, Henderson-Hasselbalch equation
- **Examples:** pH calculation, [H⁺] from pH, titration endpoint calculation, buffer behavior
- **Quiz:** 4 questions on pH logarithms, acid strength, titration stoichiometry, buffer effects
- **Depth:** Rigorous pH definitions, Ka/Kb relationships, buffer capacity

#### 9. **Chemical Equilibrium** (`chemicalEquilibriumModule`)
- **Topics:** Equilibrium constant (Kc, Kp), Le Chatelier's principle, Ksp (solubility), equilibrium shifts
- **Examples:** Kc calculation, pressure/concentration effects, solubility product, Ksp vs solubility
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on K interpretation, Le Chatelier shifts, solubility calculations
- **Depth:** Equilibrium position vs equilibrium constant, temperature effects on K

#### 10. **Thermodynamics & Enthalpy** (`thermodynamicsModule`)
- **Topics:** Heat (q), enthalpy (ΔH), calorimetry, Hess's law, entropy (ΔS), Gibbs free energy (ΔG)
- **Examples:** Calorimetry calculation, Hess's law problem solving, ΔG and spontaneity
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on heat calculations, exothermic/endothermic, Hess's law, spontaneity
- **Depth:** Enthalpy relationships, Hess's law applications, ΔG = ΔH − TΔS

### BIOLOGY (3 modules)

#### 11. **Photosynthesis: Light & Dark Reactions** (`photosynthesisModule`)
- **Topics:** Overall equation, light reactions (photosystems, ATP/NADPH), Calvin cycle (3 phases), RuBisCO
- **Examples:** Light reaction outputs, Calvin cycle ATP/NADPH requirements, carbon fixation
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on reaction location, Calvin cycle products, RuBisCO function
- **Depth:** Detailed light/dark reaction separation, glucose production stoichiometry

#### 12. **Cellular Respiration: Aerobic Pathways** (`cellularRespirationModule`)
- **Topics:** Glycolysis (net +2 ATP), pyruvate oxidation, Citric Acid Cycle (Krebs), ETC, oxidative phosphorylation, ATP yield
- **Examples:** Glycolysis ATP counting, cycle product enumeration, ETC ATP calculation, total ATP
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on glycolysis location, direct ATP production, ETC purpose
- **Depth:** Theoretical ~32 ATP yield, NADH/FADH₂ efficiency (2.5 & 1.5 ATP), substrate-level vs oxidative phosphorylation

#### 13. **DNA Replication & Protein Synthesis** (`dnaProteinSynthesisModule`)
- **Topics:** DNA structure (complementary pairing), semi-conservative replication, transcription (DNA→mRNA), translation (mRNA→protein), genetic code
- **Examples:** DNA template to mRNA, mRNA transcription, codon-to-amino acid translation
- **Quiz:** 4 questions on base pairing, transcription template, genetic code, codons
- **Depth:** Leading/lagging strand synthesis, wobble pairing, start/stop codons

---

### STATISTICS & PROBABILITY (3 modules)

#### 14. **Hypothesis Testing & Significance** (`hypothesisTestingModule`)
- **Topics:** H₀/H₁, significance level (α), test statistics (z, t), p-values, Type I/II errors, power
- **Examples:** Z-test calculation, decision rule application, error type identification
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on p-value interpretation, error types, one-tailed vs two-tailed
- **Depth:** Z-test and t-test formulas, power analysis, error trade-offs

#### 15. **Confidence Intervals** (`confidenceIntervalsModule`)
- **Topics:** Margin of error, CI formula (known σ), t-based CI (unknown σ), critical values, sample size planning
- **Examples:** 95% CI calculation, margin of error interpretation, sample size determination
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on critical values, margin of error, confidence level effects
- **Depth:** σ known vs unknown, z* vs t*, interpretation (not "probability the parameter is in the interval")

#### 16. **Linear Regression & Correlation** (`regressionModule`)
- **Topics:** Correlation coefficient (r), coefficient of determination (r²), least squares regression, slope/intercept, residuals
- **Examples:** Regression line calculation, r² interpretation, slope prediction
- **Quiz:** 3 questions on r² variance explanation, slope extraction, perfect fit
- **Depth:** SSE minimization, r ranges, slope as elasticity measure

- Clear variable definitions (μ, σ, λ, etc.)
- Step-by-step algebraic manipulations
- Explicit unit tracking (J, cm, M, Hz, etc.)
- Consistent notation across modules

### Quiz Design
